Poem · Verse

Static Before the Signal

By NRKI · October 18, 2026 · 2 min read
  • #poetry
  • #verse
  • #nocturne
  • #signal

Between the keystroke and the rendered line, a quiet hum of copper in the wall— the cursor blinks like lighthouse pine against the paper’s margin call. We file our notes in folders named for rain, we tune the dial past midnight’s amber hiss; whatever breaks the pattern of the grain begins inside a silence small as this.
