About this place

Hello, I’m the person behind NRKI.

My name is Raj, a 23 year old dev from Kullu, a small town in the mountains of India also known as Valley of the Gods.

NRKI (pronounced anarchy) is my independent digital diary of sorts, a quiet, newspaper-inspired archive for the software I build, the essays and poems I write, and the ideas I’m exploring.

Why a Newspaper?

If you think about it, most sites today look and feel the same. The more one investigates the more they realise that almost every site is in some way or form a newspaper. It's not some rule or anything, it's just the most convinient way to present information using text and media. Most sites however try to go for the same old minimalistic 'professional' aesthetic that just ends up making them feel souless and boring.

The way I see it, the only way out of this trap is to either completely rebel (rethink the way we present information) or embrace the paper and make it your own playground. The latter is what NRKI is about.

How It’s Built

The core shell is plain semantic HTML, our 4-theme broadsheet CSS library, and vanilla JavaScript with clean mount points reserved for adding React islands and standalone mini-SPAs where interactivity calls for them.

I believe, as devs we have a responsibility to balance developer convenience with creating something that is worth the user's attention.
